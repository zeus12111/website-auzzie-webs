Do I own the website?

Yes. Every package is yours to keep, with no template licence or platform you are locked into.

Why not a WordPress or Wix website?

A hand-coded site has no theme or plugins weighing it down, so it loads fast on a phone and Google can read it cleanly.

How does my website get into AI answers?

Framework adds FAQ and service schema so ChatGPT and similar tools can read your services. Full Build adds a full GEO setup for ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity.

How long does a website take?

7 days for Groundwork, 10 for Framework and 14 for Full Build.

What comes free with a Framework or Full Build website?

Framework includes 2 months of social media marketing, normally $499 a month: a $998 value. Full Build includes 3 months, a $1,497 value, plus LinkedIn management and a promo video for your business.
